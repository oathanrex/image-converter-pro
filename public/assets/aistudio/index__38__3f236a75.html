Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Image Converter Pro

Is this image converter really free?

Yes, Image Converter Pro is 100% free with no hidden costs. There's no registration required, no watermarks added, and you can convert unlimited images.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No, never. All image processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript and Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

What image formats can I convert?

Input formats: JPG/JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF (static), BMP, and SVG (rasterized)

Output formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, and PDF

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes! Batch processing is fully supported. Upload multiple images at once and convert them all with a single click.

What's the maximum file size?

You can convert images up to 50MB per file. For very large images, we recommend resizing first.

How do I convert JPG to PNG?

Simply drag and drop your JPG image, select PNG as the output format, adjust quality if needed, and click "Convert All". Then download your converted image!
